about

The first time I saw a virtual machine was at my dad's work. He pulled one up and told me it was a "computer inside a computer," and that idea never really left me. A whole machine running inside another one? I needed to know how that worked.

That's pretty much what my home lab turned into. One mini PC running Proxmox, carrying a firewall, a SIEM, a honeypot, and a few other machines across five VLANs. It started as a way to actually understand networking instead of memorizing it for a test, and now it's something I mess with almost every day: breaking it, fixing it, and automating whatever I get tired of doing by hand.

I'm big on Greek mythology, so every machine is named after whoever fits its job. Atlas is the hypervisor, because it holds up everything else. Aegis, the shield, is the firewall. Hermes, the messenger, carries the logs to the SIEM. Dolos, the spirit of trickery, runs the honeypot and lies to every attacker who logs in. Athena handles the OSINT research, and Iris delivers the alerts.

I finished my A.S. in Cybersecurity at Lanier Tech and I'm working on my B.A.S. in IT at Kennesaw State.
